How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Every guess must be a real word.

After each guess the tiles change colour to show how close you were.

  • S L A T E

    S is in the word and in the right spot.

  • C R A N E

    A is in the word but in the wrong spot.

  • P O U N D

    N is not in the word at all.

Repeated letters

A letter is only highlighted as many times as it actually appears. If the answer holds a single E and you guess two, just one of them lights up.

Two ways to play

Daily gives everyone the same word each day and keeps your streak. Practice is unlimited — a new random word whenever you want one.
