Pentaword — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Pentaword does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. It works with no internet connection.

What the app stores

Pentaword saves a small amount of information in your device's own local storage so the game works the way you would expect between sessions:

  • your statistics — games played, wins, streaks and guess distribution;
  • your settings — theme, hard mode and the high-contrast palette;
  • the puzzle you currently have in progress.

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or made available to anyone, because the app has no server component to send it to. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also clear it at any time through your device's app storage settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for any personal details.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising libraries.
  • It does not use an advertising identifier.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • It does not contain in-app purchases.
  • It makes no network requests. All of its content ships inside the app.

Sharing your result

The share button hands a short block of text — a grid of coloured squares and your score — to your device's own share sheet or clipboard. You choose where it goes. Pentaword does not send it anywhere itself, and the text never contains the answer or the words you guessed.

Android backup

Android may include the app's local storage in the device backup you have configured with Google, so your statistics can follow you to a new phone. That backup is governed by Google's privacy policy and your device settings, and is not accessible to the developer of this app. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Children

Pentaword is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects none from children either.
