Pentaword — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Pentaword is free and supported by advertising. The game itself asks for nothing about you — no account, no sign-in, no personal details — and your puzzle progress stays on your device. The ads are served by Google, and Google collects some information in order to show and measure them. That is set out in full below.

What the game stores on your device

Pentaword saves a small amount of information in your device's own storage so the game works the way you would expect between sessions:

  • your statistics — games played, wins, streaks and guess distribution;
  • your settings — theme, hard mode and the high-contrast palette;
  • the puzzle you currently have in progress.

This information stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us or made available to anyone — we operate no server and have nowhere to send it. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and you can clear it at any time through your device's app storage settings.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To select and measure them, Google collects information from this app, including:

WhatWhy
Your device's advertising IDTo select ads and limit how often you see the same one
IP address and approximate location derived from itTo serve ads relevant to your country or region
Device and app information — model, operating system version, app versionTo render ads correctly and detect invalid traffic
Ad interaction data — ads shown, tapped or completedTo measure performance and pay for the app

Google acts as an independent controller of this information. How it is used is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We receive only aggregate, non-identifying earnings reports — we never see who you are, and we cannot connect an ad to a particular player.

Your choices about ads

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent framework. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.
  • On any device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops ads being personalised to you. You will still see ads; they will simply be less relevant.
  • Declining personalised ads does not restrict the game. Every feature stays available, and nothing is withheld.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for any personal details.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • It contains no in-app purchases.
  • It does not sell your personal information.

Apart from loading ads, the game makes no network requests: the words, the puzzles and everything else ship inside the app. Pentaword remains fully playable with no internet connection — you simply will not see ads.

Sharing your result

The share button hands a short block of text — a grid of coloured squares and your score — to your device's own share sheet or clipboard. You choose where it goes. Pentaword does not send it anywhere itself, and the text never contains the answer or the words you guessed.

Android backup

Android may include the app's local storage in the device backup you have configured with Google, so your statistics can follow you to a new phone. That backup is governed by Google's privacy policy and your device settings, and is not accessible to the developer of this app. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Children

Pentaword is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you would like the advertising identifier associated with their device reset, delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which removes the identifier Google uses.
